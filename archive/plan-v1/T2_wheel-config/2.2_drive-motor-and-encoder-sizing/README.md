## What to do
Calculate the required wheel torque and speed from: total mass (robot + 100 kg payload), target speed (e.g., 0.5 m/s), acceleration, rolling resistance, small floor thresholds and a safety factor. Output the motor spec to take to the market: rated torque (N·m), rpm after gearbox, voltage, encoder resolution (CPR), shaft size, and the wheel diameter/load rating.
